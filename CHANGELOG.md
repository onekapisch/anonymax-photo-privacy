# Changelog

AnonyMax versions for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Dates are App Store release dates; versions not yet released say so.

## 1.0: coming soon to the App Store

Mac 1.0 (7) and iPhone and iPad 1.0 (15).

### Finds
- Faces, number plates, QR codes and barcodes.
- Personal text: names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, IBANs, card numbers, ID numbers, passwords, one-time and booking codes, birth dates, usernames and links.
- Other readable text, like shop signs, listed so you choose what to cover.
- Optional in Settings: whole people, documents and all text.

### Covers
- Four looks: Plain (free), and Natural blur, Mosaic and Color match with Pro.
- Every cover is built from the surroundings, never from the hidden pixels. A read-back attack runs as a leak test in every build.
- Draw a cover by hand: rectangle, ellipse or brush on the Mac, your finger on iPhone and iPad.

### Cleans and checks
- Removes the location, device and owner details, captions and keywords from the saved copy. On the Mac you can keep the date and camera settings if you want.
- A second check scans the finished copy. If something new turns up, you decide: cover it too, or keep it visible.
- Saves a covered copy; your original stays unchanged unless you choose to replace it (Mac).

### Mac
- Photos Library import, drag and drop, and the Orange Pad home screen.
- With Pro: keep the main subject visible, cover only the children, sticker face covers, whole sets at once, the notch, Finder and Shortcuts.

### iPhone and iPad
- Photo picker, Files and a share extension. Saves as HEIC, JPEG or PNG.

### Everywhere
- Private & Offline: processed on your device, works without an internet connection, no account.
- English and German.
- Free: 5 photos a month with the Plain cover. Pro: every photo, one purchase for Mac, iPhone and iPad, no subscription.
